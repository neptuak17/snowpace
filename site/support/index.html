How can I help?

Most answers are already in the Snowpace guide. Search it, or let the troubleshooter find the right page in a tap or two.

    The troubleshooterAnswer a question or two and get to the page that solves it. The whole guideEvery screen, how scores work, where the data comes from, and your privacy.

    Common questions

    • A score doesn't match how the day skied
    • The hill has snow, but Snowpace says it's bare
    • My ski area is missing, or listed with the wrong activities
    • There's no webcam for my place, or the wrong one
    • A place shows no forecast, or an old one
    • Can I change what counts as a good day?
    • Does Snowpace know whether the hill is open or groomed?
    • What does Snowpace do with my data, and how do I delete it?
    • More questions and answers

    Get in touch

    support@snowpace.com

    For a score that looks wrong, send it from the app. Tap Not right? under the verdict, on Today, on any place, or on any day in Forecast. Pick what's off, and the app can attach that day's forecast and score, so I can see exactly what you saw. You read the email before it goes, and it's sent from your own Mail app.

    For anything else, tap the ? at the top of Today or any other main tab, then Send feedback at the bottom.

    Snowpace is a one-person project. I read everything, but a reply can take a few days.
