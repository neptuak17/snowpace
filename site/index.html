Is it worth going out today?

Have you ever spent the morning checking recent precipitation and what happened weather-wise in the past week, checking current and forecast temperatures, wind and snow conditions? Surfing webcams to get a better idea of what is happening at the hills? Snowpace instantly does all of that and more for all of the locations you may visit, wrapping it up into a simple to understand verdict, backed by data.

How it decides

Eight things go into every score

Each one is weighted for the activity. Four of them are measured against what you tell the app: your ideal temperature, how much wind you'll put up with, how much fresh snow you want, and how you feel about snow falling while you're out.

What else you'll find

The best hours

Today, hour by hour, with the best three hours in a row marked. Tap an hour for its temperature, wind and any snow or rain.

Five days ahead

Forecast lays out every place you've saved against the next five days. Tap any square for the detail on that day.

Better nearby

On a poor day at your home hill, places nearby that score better are listed right under it, with how far away they are.

See it for yourself

Where a place has a webcam of its own, a recent picture from windy.com sits under the hours. It's there to look at and never changes a score.

Private by design

There's no Snowpace server to send anything to

The app talks straight to the public services it uses, and only asks them about ski areas.

  • No accountNothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackingNone are built in, and none will be.
  • Your location stays on your phoneIt's used to show how far away each place is and is never sent anywhere.
  • Public data, creditedForecasts from Open-Meteo, ski areas from OpenSkiData, webcam pictures from windy.com.

Snowpace only scores the weather

It doesn't know whether lifts are running or trails are groomed, so every place links to its operator's web site. Check it, and the avalanche forecast for the area, before you go.
