## Description (4000)

    Is it worth going out today?

    Snowpace reads the mountain forecast for the places you ski and gives each day a score out of 100 for the way you ski it: downhill, classic, skate or snowshoe. It does the morning's homework for you: last week's snow and rain, today's temperature, wind and new snow, and what the snowpack has been through. Then it sums it up in a verdict you can read at a glance.

    MADE FOR THE WAY YOU SKI
    A powder day that makes downhill great bogs down a skate ski. Every place gets its own score for each activity, measured against your own preferences for temperature, wind and new snow.

    THE ANSWER, AND WHY
    • Today, hour by hour, with the best three hours in a row marked
    • The reason in a sentence, and every factor behind the score one tap away
    • Five days ahead for every place you save
    • On a poor day at your home hill, places nearby that score better
    • A recent daylight webcam picture, where a place has one

    OVER 1,300 PLACES ACROSS NORTH AMERICA
    Downhill hills, nordic centres and snowshoe trails in Canada and the United States, from OpenStreetMap and Skimap.org. Search by name or town and save the places you go.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your places and settings stay on your phone. Location is optional: it only shows how far away each place is, and it never leaves your phone.

    SNOWPACE ONLY SCORES THE WEATHER
    It doesn't know whether lifts are running or trails are groomed, so every place links to its operator's web site. Check it, and the avalanche forecast for the area, before you go.

    Free, with nothing to buy. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. Ski areas © OpenStreetMap contributors and Skimap.org, via OpenSkiData. Webcams provided by windy.com.

## Age rating

Every answer is None / No, which gives 4+. The one to think about:
"Unrestricted web access" is **No**. The app opens fixed links (an operator's
web site, the guide, a webcam's page on windy.com) in the in-app browser or
Safari; it has no address bar or open browsing of its own.

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Location is read on the phone and never sent. Forecast
and webcam requests carry ski-area coordinates only. Feedback is an email the
user writes and sends from their own Mail app, optional and infrequent, which
Apple lets an app leave off the label ("optional disclosure"). No tracking, so
no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

## App Review notes

    Snowpace needs no account or sign-in.

    It covers ski areas in Canada and the United States only. Today suggests other places only within 150 km of the phone, so from outside a ski region it may show few suggestions. To see the app working: on the My places tab, tap "+ Add", search for "Whistler Blackcomb" (or any North American ski area), tap "Add", then "Set home". Today then opens on that place.

    Location is optional. It is used only on the phone, to show distances and sort search results; the app works fully without it.

    Data comes from public services, called directly from the phone: forecasts from Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), ski areas from OpenSkiData (tiles.openskimap.org, at most once every 30 days), and webcam stills from Windy (api.windy.com; stills only, no player and no ads). The webcams can be switched off on the You tab.

    In the off-season, or where the forecast shows no snow on the ground, scores are low by design and the card says the ground is bare.

    Contact: support@snowpace.com
