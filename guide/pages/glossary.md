---
title: Glossary
description: The words Snowpace and this guide use, in plain terms.
---
**Activity.** Downhill, classic, skate or snowshoe. Each has its own score and settings.

**Band.** The word and colour for a range of scores: {{ui.bands.hi}}, {{ui.bands.go}}, {{ui.bands.fair}}, {{ui.bands.poor}}, {{ui.bands.skip}}. See [The score](/guide/the-score/).

**Best hours.** The best-scoring {{tune.hourly.windowHours}} hours in a row today. See [Best hours](/guide/best-hours/).

**Crust.** Snow that melted and refroze hard. See [Freeze–thaw](/guide/freeze-thaw/).

**Factor.** One of the eight weather measures behind a score, such as wind or new snow.

**Forecast age.** How long ago the forecast for a place was fetched, shown under its answer.

**Freeze–thaw.** The factor for crust and slush, over the day and the two before it.

**GEM.** The Canadian government's weather models, used for Canadian places.

**Holding it back.** In **{{ui.why.show}}**, the factors pulling a day's score down, the costliest first. See [The Why? detail](/guide/why/).

**Home hill.** The place **{{ui.tabs.today}}** opens on. Set it with **{{ui.search.setHome}}**.

**Modelled.** Worked out by a weather model rather than measured, like the snowpack.

**Slush.** Snow that melted and stayed above freezing.

**Snowpack.** The modelled snow depth. When it's holding the score down, **{{ui.why.show}}** says {{ui.coverage.thin}} or {{ui.coverage.bare}}.

**Surface.** For classic and skate, a word for the likely snow surface, such as {{ui.surface.words.firm}}. Not part of the score yet. See [The Surface row](/guide/surface/).
