## {{ui.you.activitiesKicker}}

Switch on the activities you do. Only those appear anywhere in the app.

## Your preferences, for each activity

Pick an activity with the chips, then set:

- **{{ui.you.idealTemp}}.** {{ui.you.idealTempHint}}
- **{{ui.you.windLimit}}.** {{ui.you.windLimitHint}}
- **New snow:** called **{{ui.snowPref.labelDownhill}}** for downhill, **{{ui.snowPref.labelSnowshoe}}** for snowshoeing, and **{{ui.snowPref.labelNordic}}** for classic and skate.
- **Snow falling:** **{{ui.fall.labelOut}}** for downhill and snowshoeing, **{{ui.fall.labelTolerance}}** for classic and skate.

[Your settings](/guide/your-settings/) explains how each one changes a score, and lists the values Snowpace starts with.
