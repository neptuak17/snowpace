## {{ui.verdictFeedback.title}}

At the foot of every answer, on **{{ui.tabs.today}}**, a place, or any day in **{{ui.tabs.forecast}}**, **{{ui.verdictFeedback.title}}** opens a short sheet for saying the verdict is off.

1. It shows the verdict you're reporting, so you can see it while you answer.
2. Under **{{ui.verdictFeedback.issuesKicker}}**, tap any that apply: "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.high}}", "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.low}}", "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.reason}}", "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.weather}}" or "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.other}}". On a bare or thin day there's also "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.snow}}", and when best hours were shown, "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.hours}}".
3. Leave **{{ui.feedback.diagnosticsLabel}}** on to bring along the day's forecast and what was behind its score.
4. Tap **{{ui.verdictFeedback.send}}**. Mail opens a draft to {{contact.email}}. Say what you saw at the hill, and send it from your own account.

**{{ui.common.cancel}}**, or a swipe down, closes the sheet without sending anything.

## Anything else

**{{ui.common.help}}** (the round **?** at the top of any main tab), then **{{ui.help.feedbackButton}}**, for something broken, an idea, or anything else. Pick what it's about and, for a score, which place.

## What's in the email

Your own words, then, if diagnostics are on: the app and iOS versions, your device model, your activities and settings, when data was last fetched, how long the app took to start, and for a place, its forecast and score, with the forecast attached as a file. Never your location, and never your other places. You see everything before it's sent, and can delete any of it. See [Privacy and your data](/guide/privacy-and-data/#feedback-you-send).

If Mail isn't set up on your phone, see [Can't send feedback](/guide/cant-send-feedback/).
