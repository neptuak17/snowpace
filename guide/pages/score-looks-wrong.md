---
title: A score looks wrong
description: The usual reasons a score doesn't match the day, and how to tell which one it is.
---
Start with **{{ui.why.show}}** under the answer: the emptiest bar is usually the reason, and the sentence under the dial names it.

## Check these first

1. **The activity.** The chips at the top choose it, and the same weather scores very differently for each.
2. **Your settings.** Snowpace starts everyone with the same [settings](/guide/your-settings/). If you like it colder, or don't mind wind, change them on the **{{ui.tabs.you}}** tab.
3. **The forecast's age,** shown under the answer. An old one can be well out of date. See [No forecast, or an old one](/guide/no-forecast/#an-old-forecast).
4. **The day.** In **{{ui.tabs.forecast}}**, check you tapped the right square.

## There is snow, but it says bare

The snowpack comes from a weather model's estimate, and the model doesn't know about snowmaking or grooming. Early and late in the season, a hill making snow can show "{{ui.verdict.bareGround}}" while its runs are open. Look at the webcam, if there is one, and the place's web site. Then tap **{{ui.verdictFeedback.title}}** and choose "{{ui.verdictFeedback.issues.snow}}": those reports are how Snowpace's snowpack readings get tuned.

## It felt better, or worse, than the score

- **Grooming and preparation** aren't in any data. A well-groomed trail can ski far better than the weather suggests.
- **The forecast was off.** See [How accurate is it?](/guide/weather-forecasts/#how-accurate-is-it)
- **Elevation.** Downhill scores use the average of base and summit; your runs may be colder or warmer.
- **Crust, slush or ice.** See [Freeze–thaw](/guide/freeze-thaw/) and [Rain and ice](/guide/rain-and-ice/).
- **A big snowfall on skate or classic.** Deep new snow holds the score down until the trails are packed. See [Deep new snow](/guide/snowpack-and-deep-snow/#deep-new-snow).

## Tell me

If the score still looks wrong, **{{ui.verdictFeedback.title}}** under it sends me that exact day: the forecast, every factor, and what you picked as off. Scores are tuned from reports like these.
