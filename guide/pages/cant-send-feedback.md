---
title: Can't send feedback
description: What to do when the feedback button doesn't open Mail.
---
Feedback opens a draft in Apple's Mail app, addressed to {{contact.email}}, and you send it from your own account. That needs Mail set up with an email account on your phone.

## Mail isn't set up

The feedback screen says "{{ui.feedback.unavailableKicker}}" and shows the address instead. Email {{contact.email}} from any other email app, or from a computer. It helps to say which place and day you mean, and what you saw.

## The draft disappeared

If you closed the draft without sending, Mail may have kept it in **Drafts**. Snowpace says "Saved as a draft in Mail" when it was.

## Nothing happened after Send

Check Mail's **Outbox**: without a connection, Mail sends it once you're back online.
