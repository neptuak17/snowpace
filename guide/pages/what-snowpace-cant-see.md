---
title: What Snowpace can't see
description: The things that decide a day that no weather forecast knows.
---
Snowpace scores the weather. Several things that make or break a day aren't in any forecast:

- **Whether it's open.** Hours, lifts and trails are only known to the operator, and only sold through commercial feeds. Where the listing has one, each place links to its own web site: check it before you go.
- **Grooming.** No public data says when a trail was last groomed.
- **Snowmaking.** The snowpack comes from a weather model that doesn't know about it, so a hill making snow can show as bare. See [Snowpack](/guide/snowpack-and-deep-snow/).
- **Avalanche conditions.** Check the avalanche forecast for the area.
- **Your exact spot.** The forecast is for the area's location: for downhill, the average of base and summit; for nordic, mid elevation. A big area varies with height, aspect and shelter.
- **What actually happened.** A score is a forecast, not a report. Each place shows how old its forecast is.

Distances are straight lines from where you are, not driving routes, and Snowpace never estimates drive times.
