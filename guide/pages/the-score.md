Every saved place gets a score from 0 to 100 for each of your activities, for today and the next four days. It says how good the **weather** is for that activity, measured against what you said you like. It knows nothing about whether the place is open or groomed.

{{table:bands}}

The band sets the dial's colour and the word beside it. At {{derived.bands.everything}} or more, the sentence under the dial says so: "{{ui.verdict.excellent}}"

## One place, four scores

The same weather can be great for one activity and poor for another: fresh snow that makes a downhill day bogs down a skate ski. So every place has a separate score for each activity. The chips at the top of **{{ui.tabs.today}}** and the place screen show each one; tap a chip to switch, and the whole app follows.

## When there's no score

A dash ({{ui.common.dash}}) means there's nothing to score: no forecast for that day yet, or a gap in it. Snowpace never fills a gap with a guess. See [No forecast, or an old one](/guide/no-forecast/).

## What goes into it

Eight weather factors, weighted for your activity, then capped by the snowpack. [How scores work](/guide/how-scores-work/) walks through each step, and **{{ui.why.show}}** under any answer shows how each factor scored that day.
