Snowpace is a one-person project. I read everything, but a reply can take a few days, so it's worth a quick look first:

1. **Search this guide,** or try the [troubleshooter](/guide/troubleshooting/).
2. **For a score that looks wrong,** tap **{{ui.verdictFeedback.title}}** under it. It sends the exact day, forecast and score, so I can see what you saw.
3. **For anything else in the app,** **{{ui.common.help}}** → **{{ui.help.feedbackButton}}**. With **{{ui.feedback.diagnosticsLabel}}** on, it brings the details I'd otherwise have to ask for.

## Email

[{{contact.email}}](mailto:{{contact.email}}?subject=Snowpace)

It helps to say which place, which day and which activity, and what you saw compared with what Snowpace said. A screenshot helps too.
